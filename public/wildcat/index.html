Wildcat Glass has been glazing storefronts, curtainwall and entrances across southern Missouri for well over a decade, including years of work for Mercy. What I found

Your current site, as a visitor sees it

  • Security

    New visitors now get a warning page before your site opens. Your site has no secure (https) version. Starting with version 154 this fall, Google Chrome tries the secure version first and, when it isn't there, warns before opening a site the visitor hasn't been to before. Brave does the same. A contractor looking you up for the first time is exactly that visitor, and has to click "Continue to site" to see you.

  • Projects

    Your best work is buried. The Gallery has 13 good job photos, including storefronts like Discount Tire in Springfield, but they sit in a slideshow with two captions and no job details. Nothing on the site shows your work for Mercy, which your About page calls one of your main customers.

  • Wording

    Text left over from the template. The homepage headline reads "Creative vision, bold design." The "Our Team" section shows stock photos instead of your crew. The mission statement on the Contact page is about architecture, and the "Our portfolio," "Awards and honors" and "Read our blog" links go nowhere.

  • Out of date

    Old numbers. The site says you've been in business 13 years, in two places, and that Steve has 40 years in the trade. Your domain dates to 2010, and 1983 was 43 years ago.

  • Bid requests

    The quote form asks people to type their company, name, address and phone into the message box because it has no fields for them. There's no way to send plans.
