Website proposal

A website that shows the commercial work you already do

Wildcat Glass has been glazing storefronts, curtainwall and entrances across southern Missouri for well over a decade, including years of work for Mercy. Right now, a contractor or facility manager who looks you up finds a four-page site with no photos and none of that work on it. This proposal fixes that for one flat price.

What I found

Your current site, as a visitor sees it

  • Photos

    When I tested in Google Chrome, none of your photos loaded. The logo, the main photo and every photo in the Gallery come up blank. The photos are still stored at GoDaddy, but the old website builder serves them in a way Chrome refuses.

  • Security

    No secure connection. Browsers label the site "Not secure," and typing https:// in front of your address gives a certificate error instead of your site.

  • Wording

    Text left over from the template. The headline reads "Creative vision, bold design." The mission statement on the Contact page is about architects. There are links for "Awards and honors" and "Read our blog" that go nowhere.

  • Phones

    Built for desktops. Phones get a separate stripped-down version, and your phone number in the page text can't be tapped to call.

  • Out of date

    Old facts. The site says you've been in business 13 years in two places. Your domain dates to 2010.

  • Bid requests

    The contact form asks people to type their company, name, address and phone into the message box because the form has no fields for them. There's no way to attach plans.
